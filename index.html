Manuscripts Under Review Preprints forthcoming on arXiv

  1. A. Zandi, S. Nami, R. Ganjipour, B. Sabagh Kermani, A. Tarabkhah, H. Farbeh. Real-Time Obstacle Detection and Distance Estimation on Smartphones for Visually Impaired and Distracted Pedestrians. Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems.

    My role: contributed to implementing the early-exit heads, and implemented ARCore depth-based distance estimation with a monocular focal-length fallback and the on-device benchmarks; the compressed YOLOv8n ran up to 8× faster on a mid-range phone (3.2 s → 0.39 s), recommended setup at 0.42–0.48 s with 0.81 recall.

  2. A. Zandi, A. Azami, B. Sabagh Kermani, P. Abbasian, R. Ganjipour, S. Nami, H. Farbeh. ROD-Dataset: A Pedestrian-Perspective Benchmark of 25 Urban Obstacle Classes for Real-Time Detection. Under review.

    24,326 images and 40,195 annotated instances across 25 classes, merging 29 public collections with street-level imagery captured in Tehran and Toronto. Benchmarked six YOLO generations (v8–v26), showing recall and mAP rankings disagree; open source (MIT) with 88K+ Hugging Face downloads.

    My role: collected and annotated part of the field-captured imagery.

  3. A. Zandi, A. Ashrafi, S. Nami, S. S. Neghahban, A. Entezari, P. Abbasian, A. Azami, P. Taherzadeh, B. Sabagh Kermani, R. Ganjipour, H. Farbeh. From White Canes to Edge AI: A Survey of Assistive Navigation Technologies for Blind and Low-Vision Individuals.

    PRISMA-based review of 108 studies (2008–2026) in a four-era taxonomy; contributed the literature review of lightweight YOLO methods (pruning, quantization, attention) for its edge-optimization synthesis.
